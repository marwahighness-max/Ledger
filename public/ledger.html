Ledger UnoBank

See every place your flow asks before it has earned it

Ledger maps what each screen asks of the user (data, permission, belief, money, attention) against what the product has given them by that point. Where the ask runs ahead, it tells you, and the team decides what to do.

01Reads your flowSteps from the funnel map, screens from Studio, baseline trust from Anchor.
02Weighs each askAgainst what the user has received so far. A green or red balance at every step.
03You decideEvery finding is a proposal. Nothing in the flow changes without a person.
How the score works

Exchange is 60%: the share of asks made from a positive balance. Decisions is 25%: the share of findings a person has resolved, including "keep as built" with a reason. Measured is 15%: the share of heavy asks with a proxy signal attached. Earned (80+) is only possible when no step is in debt, so a flow in debt caps at 79 however many decisions it has. Ask weights and the baseline are project decisions, set in Anchor and editable per project. The score reflects the team's judgment, not an objective measure of trust.
